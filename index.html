Meeting preparation and the working day

AI at Work

Rob Koch · 40 slides

Sign-language AI and its limits, the wider costs and risks of AI, three meeting scenarios, reusable prompts and a hypothetical interpreter’s day across Seattle.

Key takeaways

  • Sign-language recognition and generation are not ready to replace human interpreters.
  • AI preparation should account for privacy, accuracy, environmental costs, and other real-world risks.
  • Three meeting scenarios show how preparation changes with the people, setting, and purpose.
  • Reusable prompts can turn public research into a focused interpreter brief.
  • Interpreters should verify AI output and use only information approved for the assignment.
