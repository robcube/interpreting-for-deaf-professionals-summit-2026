## Starter terminology — validate against the actual agenda

- EDA — event-driven architecture: services communicate through events representing changes, such as an order being placed. [4]

- Kubernetes / K8s — an open-source platform for managing containerized applications and services; K8s abbreviates Kubernetes. [5]

- These are preparation definitions, not prescribed signs. Confirm meeting-specific meanings, product names, acronyms, and agreed terminology with Rob and the interpreting team.

## Questions before the meeting

- Ask the organizer: What decision is being made, who is attending, and can we receive the agenda, slides, diagrams, and acronym list through an approved channel?

- Ask Rob: What is your role in this meeting, and which points or technical distinctions must come through accurately?

- Ask Rob and the interpreting team: Which language, terminology, fingerspelling, clarification signals, pacing, and turn-taking arrangements work for this meeting?

- Confirm the room or video layout, visibility of shared diagrams, and team-interpreter coordination. Do not infer access preferences from a biography.
