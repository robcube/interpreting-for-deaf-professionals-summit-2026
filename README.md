# Interpreting for Deaf Professionals Summit 2026

Talks and audience resources shared by Rob Koch.

Presented at the 2026 Interpreting for Deaf Professionals Summit, organized by [2axend](https://2axend.com/idpsummit/). For the full session recordings, visit the [2axend Hub](https://hub.2axend.com/). Recordings are not included in this repository.

**[Open the talks and resources website](https://robcube.github.io/interpreting-for-deaf-professionals-summit-2026/)**

## AI at Work
Rob Koch. A presenter-led session on using AI to prepare for meetings with Deaf professionals.

- [PowerPoint: AI at Work](ai-at-work/AI-at-Work_2026_CC-BY.pptx) — 40 slides, including the state of sign-language AI and the wider costs and risks of AI.
- [Interpreter day planner](https://robcube.github.io/interpreting-for-deaf-professionals-summit-2026/ai-at-work/Interpreter-Day-Planner.html)
- [AI alternatives](https://robcube.github.io/interpreting-for-deaf-professionals-summit-2026/ai-at-work/Interpreter-Day-Planner.html#alternatives)
- [Copy-paste audience prompts](ai-at-work/Audience-Prompt-Handout.md)
- [Plan your interpreting day: reusable prompt](ai-at-work/Copy-Paste-Prompt_Plan-My-Interpreting-Day.md)
- [Saved public-source interpreter brief about Rob](ai-at-work/Rob-Koch_Saved-Interpreter-Brief_2026-09-26.md)
- [Illustrative ChatGPT walkthrough](https://robcube.github.io/interpreting-for-deaf-professionals-summit-2026/ai-at-work/ChatGPT-Demo-Backup.html)

Maya and Aisha are fictional. Rob is real, but the meeting is hypothetical. The research brief is a training draft, not confirmation of communication preferences. The walkthrough is staged illustration, not a captured live AI response. The day planner has no live traffic feed or AI connection.

## Networked for Success
Rob Koch and Jordann Hoelzel. Professional networking, access agreements and coordination between Deaf professionals and interpreters.

- [PowerPoint: Networked for Success](networking/Networked-for-Success_2026_CC-BY.pptx) — 22 slides, based on the approved Team Clarity v6 deck.

Both public decks retain speaker notes and source citations. Creative Commons notices appear on their closing slides. Earlier drafts and internal build files are not included.

## Using the files
Download the repository ZIP to keep the HTML files and demo images together. Open `index.html` in a browser. The planner calculations work locally; external source links need internet access. The pages do not transmit assignment data or connect to AI services.
